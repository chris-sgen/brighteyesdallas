# brighteyesdallas

> **Unofficial copy — not the website of Bright Eyes Vision.** This repository is a development copy of
> a redesign, published so that the redesign can be looked at in a browser. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by
> Bright Eyes Vision. The practice's real site is https://www.brighteyesdallas.com/.
>
> **A snapshot, and none of the practice's forms sends anything here.** The pages were read from the practice's site on the evening of October 19, 2026, Dallas time (October 20, between 01:29 and 01:40 UTC), and are
> not kept current. The practice's contact form is disabled in this copy (its page says so and gives the practice's telephone number), and the appointment form and the patient registration form, which another company draws on the live site for the practice's own account, are not loaded (each of those pages says so and links to the practice's own page).

**Preview: https://chris-sgen.github.io/brighteyesdallas/**

A **redesign** of **brighteyesdallas.com** (Bright Eyes Vision, an eye care practice in Dallas, Texas): 207 pages, made by a rebuild pipeline and changed for public hosting as listed under *Hardening*: the 204 pages of the live site at their own addresses, and a search page, a sitemap page and a not-found page that the build makes itself. It is a static site with no backend. To look at it, use the preview link. To serve the folder yourself, use a server that answers a folder's address (`/insurance/`) with the `index.html` inside it, under the path `/brighteyesdallas/` (the not-found page names its files there).

> **13 still pictures and 1 silent video loop in this copy are AI-generated illustrations made for this redesign.**
> They are not photographs of the practice, its office, its doctors or its staff, and none shows a person (see *Generated imagery*).

Two terms used below. The **private build** is the build of this redesign that this copy was made from,
with its documentation and audit files; it is not published. This **preview** is that build with the
changes listed under *Hardening*.

## Hardening applied to this public copy

`PROVENANCE.json` gives the counts.

1. **`noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet, noimageindex` on every page.** This tag is the only index control this copy has. It is a request, not a lock: the large search engines say they follow `noindex`; `noimageindex` is a rule of Google's. A `robots.txt` with `Disallow: /` is also shipped, but it has **no effect** here: crawlers read `robots.txt` only at the host root (`chris-sgen.github.io/robots.txt`, which only a separate repository for that host could provide), not under `/brighteyesdallas/`. GitHub Pages cannot send an `X-Robots-Tag` header either. That leaves every file that is not a page (the pictures, the loop, the fonts, the stylesheets, the script, the search index, the licence texts and these documents) with no index control of its own; on this site they are linked only from pages marked `nofollow`. The repository itself is public: github.com lists every file of it, and its page there can be indexed.
2. **Link-preview tags.** `og:url` points at this preview, `og:description` / `twitter:description` carry the disclosure, `og:site_name` reads "Unofficial preview (not Bright Eyes Vision)", and `og:title` / `twitter:title` begin "Unofficial preview:" (207 pages), with the practice's name taken off where it stands at the end of the title ("… | Bright Eyes Vision"). The picture tags (`og:image`, `twitter:image`: 414 tags) are removed and `twitter:card` is `summary`, the small card, so no tag offers a picture. The practice's icon is not this copy's tab or home-screen icon (414 icon links removed; an empty icon stands in). **The `<title>` of a page is not marked**: it is the private build's title. What a link card shows depends on the app: apps that take a link's description or site name from these tags show the disclosure; apps that read none of these tags show the page's own title with the `chris-sgen.github.io` address; apps that pick a picture from the page itself may show one of its pictures.
3. **JSON-LD removed** (619 blocks). It asserted, as data for search engines, the practice's identity, address, telephone and opening hours, and the trail and kind of each page.
4. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` on every page, so a click on an outbound link does not tell its target this address. The 206 map frames carry `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` themselves, because a policy on a frame overrules the page's. No page holds a film; the script's code for a film frame (unused here) would send only this site's bare origin.
5. **The practice's form is disabled, with or without JavaScript** (the contact form, `/contact-us/contact-form/`). Three safeguards are layered:
    - **A hidden, disabled submit button is placed first in the form**, followed by a hidden, disabled text field. A form whose default button is disabled is not submitted when Enter is pressed in it; a browser that skips a disabled button decides by counting the form's single-line fields, and the hidden field keeps that count above one (that second case was not tested here: only Chrome was run).
    - The **submit button is replaced by a disabled `type="button"`**.
    - The form gets `action=""`, `onsubmit="return false"`, `method="dialog"` and `data-preview="inert"`, and the hooks by which the private build's script finds a form are removed, so that script does not touch it at all.

    A note in the head of the page says that the form on the page is disabled and gives the practice's telephone number; a shorter line right above the form and a line under its button say it again. The fields can still be typed in; nothing typed leaves the browser. **The site search still works**: its 211 forms send the words only to this copy's own `/search/` page, as part of that page's address.
6. **The forms and the badge another company draws for the practice's own account are not loaded.** On the live site the appointment form (`/contact-us/schedule-appointment/`) and the patient registration form (`/contact-us/patient-registration-form/`) are drawn by a script of `cdn.reviewwave.com` with the practice's own ids, and a badge of patient reviews from the same company stands in a corner of every page (the private build shows it on wide screens). Here the two forms' scripts are removed, with the bands that held them, and a note in the head of each page says that the form is not loaded and links to the practice's own page; the badge's settings are taken off every page (621 attributes on 207 pages), so the page's script asks that company for nothing. The "Schedule Appointment" buttons of this copy lead to its `/contact-us/schedule-appointment/` page and its note.
7. **The portraits of the practice's two doctors are not shown.** In their place stands the tile with the doctor's initials that the private build itself draws for a member whose portrait is not on file (8 places on 4 pages: `/`, `/our-eye-doctors/`, `/team/dr-evangeli-inocando/`, `/team/dr-yuvani-tran/`). The doctors' names and biographies are the live site's and are carried (see *Known limits*). The other photographs of people in this copy are stock photographs and makers' advertising pictures; every distinct picture was looked at before publishing, on contact sheets.
8. **No on-page disclosure banner.** The disclosure is carried by the link-preview tags (in apps that show them; see 2) and by this README. Apart from the notes named here a page looks like a page of the practice: its logo, its address, telephone number and hours, and its words. 11 pages carry a note of this preview in their head, under their title: the contact form's page (see 5); the appointment and registration pages (see 6); the page whose document link now leads to the live site (see 9); and 7 pages whose own words speak about the live website, its forms or its services (`/disclaimer/`, `/privacy-policy/`, `/aoa-privacy-policy/`, `/website-accessibility-policy/`, `/welcome-to-our-new-website/`, `/contact-us/`, `/insurance/carecredit/`), whose note says that the words on the page are the live site's own and describe the practice's live website and its services, not this preview, which is a separate redesign. Each note speaks of "this preview"; none says "unofficial".
9. **Links.** The credit application link of `/insurance/carecredit/` carried a referral code of the credit company (`?dtc=…`; the same code stands on another practice's site of the same web platform, so whose code it is cannot be told from here): here it leads to the practice's own page about that credit, `https://www.brighteyesdallas.com/insurance/carecredit/`. The practice's own document, a PDF handout ("Advanced Wellness Screening Options"), is not shipped: its link on `/eye-care-services-old/2713-2/` leads to the practice's own page that offers it, and a note in the head of that page says so. No link of this copy carries a campaign mark or a referral code.
10. **`<link rel="canonical">` names the live address of the page**, the usual signal for a duplicate, and deliberately different from `og:url` (204 pages). 3 pages have none: `/404.html`, `/search/`, `/sitemap/`, which the build makes itself (the live site answers `/sitemap` with its not-found page). **Description tags** are the private build's, except on 4 pages, where the disclosure stands instead because the description promised an online form or a HIPAA-compliant form that this copy does not have: `/contact-us/contact-form/`, `/contact-us/`, `/contact-us/patient-registration-form/`, `/contact-us/schedule-appointment/`.
11. **Files left out, files added.** `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` are not shipped, because they advertise the practice's real addresses to crawlers; nor is the `_redirects` file, which GitHub Pages ignores (the forwards of the private build from old addresses of the live site do not exist here, and such an address shows the not-found page). 16 files of the private build that no page, stylesheet or script of this copy names are not shipped (sizes of pictures that only the link-preview tags or the icon links named, and the sizes of the doctors' portraits). The comments of the stylesheet and of the script, notes of the private build to whoever maintains it, are taken out (181 and 105 comments); the script was checked to parse without them. A helper of the private build's tooling that 206 of the 207 pages carried in their head (it rewrites picture addresses held in inline styles, and no page of this copy has one; `404.html` never carried it) is taken out, and so is an editor's private data block of the one SVG drawing. Added: `assets/css/preview.css` (the styles of the notes and of the disabled button), the licence texts of the 2 typefaces beside the font files, `.nojekyll` (GitHub Pages serves the files as they are), `.gitattributes` (no line endings are rewritten) and `.gitignore`.
12. `404.html` names its files under `/brighteyesdallas/`, because GitHub Pages serves it for an unknown address at any depth.
13. **The search index** is the private build's file with these changes: the entries of the 3 pages that hold the form or held another company's form keep only their title and section, and 1 summary that promised an online form is blanked (`/contact-us/`).

## Known limits

- **None of the practice's forms sends anything here, and nothing can be booked or registered.** See Hardening 5 and 6. This copy's pages and script set no cookie and keep nothing in the browser (measured locally, with the map frames blocked); the Google map frames are Google's, and what they do is Google's. The host, GitHub Pages, receives every request as any web host does and keeps its own logs.
- **This is a snapshot.** The pages were read on the evening of October 19, 2026, Dallas time (October 20, between 01:29 and 01:40 UTC); their pictures and stylesheets were downloaded by 01:48 UTC, and the live pages were then loaded in a browser until 02:56 UTC, to measure their look and to see which words their scripts add. Opening hours, insurance plans, brands and services stand as the live site showed them then. **The top bar says "Today" and gives that day's hours from the snapshot, and the hours tables mark that day**: the day is the one of the visitor's own clock, not Dallas time, and on a holiday or after a change of hours the line is wrong. The practice's own site is the place for what holds today.
- **The words on the pages are the live site's, and some of them speak about the live site or invite a visitor to use it** ("fill out the form", "our website"). The pages named in Hardening 8 carry a note; elsewhere the practice's words are carried as written.
- **90 pages are articles of the live site's eye-health library.** The live site keeps every one of them out of search engines (`noindex`), as it does 130 of its rebuilt pages in all; here every page is `noindex` anyway.
- **The generated pictures and the loop are illustrations, not the practice's photographs.** No caption or frame on the pages tells them apart; `PROVENANCE.md` lists each one.
- **The video loop has no pause control.** It is silent and hidden from screen readers, it plays only on or near the screen, and when the device asks for reduced motion or data saving it does not play at all and its first frame shows instead. WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) asks for a way to pause moving content that starts by itself and lasts more than five seconds; this design does not provide one.
- **People.** The two doctors' names, credentials and biographies are carried as the live site prints them, without their portraits (Hardening 7); a biography includes personal details the live site publishes (where the doctor runs, her family). The 5 patient reviews are carried as the live site prints them, each signed with a short name (a first name or a nickname with an initial, a title with an initial, or a user name) and a place in Dallas, on their own pages and together on the home page; they belong to their authors.
- **Links that lead to live services.** 1,371 links dial the practice's telephone number. 1 mail link opens a message to `billing@brighteyesdallas.com` (on `/website-accessibility-policy/`). 213 links (207 × "Order Contact Lenses" on 207 pages, 6 × "Order Online" on 6 pages) open the practice's contact-lens store at `yourlens.com`, a service of another company for the practice's account: what a visitor does there is real and reaches that store and the practice, not this copy. The notes of Hardening 6 and 9, the credit link and the document link open the practice's own site.
- **Links to other organisations.** `google.com` (412: directions to the practice on Google Maps); `facebook.com` (207: the practice's Facebook page); `instagram.com` (207: the practice's Instagram page); `healthgrades.com` (207: a review profile of Dr. Tran ("Healthgrades" in the footer)); `ecp.eyeglassguide.com` (41: "Visit the EyeGlass Guide today!" after the credit lines); `optos.com` (1: the maker of a retinal camera ("Optos website")); `infantsee.org` (1: a programme of infant eye exams); `latisse.com` (1: the site of an eyelash treatment, Latisse); `eyeglassguide.com` (1: the same invitation on one page); `carecredit.com` (1: how the credit company's financing works ("[Click for Details]")); `get.adobe.com` (1: the download page of a PDF reader). The links to the EyeGlass Guide (42 on 39 pages, 23 of them on library articles) follow its credit lines. The 206 map frames (in the "Hours & Location" band before the footer, or in the location pages' own band) load from Google Maps when they near the screen, and the browser then talks to Google's servers; the frames carry `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` (Hardening 4).
- **Nothing forwards an old or misspelled address** (Hardening 11); such an address shows the not-found page. How the host treats capitals and a missing closing slash had not yet been measured at the public address when this file was written.
- **File names.** A picture's file is named after the name the live site gave it (in small letters, shortened), with a short code added; some names are the live site's random codes, and some keep a picture agency's number. What a page says in its text, in the descriptions of its pictures and in the tags of its head was read for the names of the live site's system; the addresses of its files were not.
- **Tested in Chrome 155 (headless, on Windows) only.** Firefox and Safari were not run. Phone and tablet figures are emulated widths, not devices.
- **Commit metadata is public**: the author's name and e-mail address, and the commit times.
